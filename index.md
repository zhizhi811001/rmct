# Regional Manufacturing Capability Taxonomy

The Regional Manufacturing Capability Taxonomy (RMCT) is a structured knowledge organisation system for representing and organising the manufacturing capabilities of organisations across production processes, materials, quality and certification, engineering and industrialisation, digital production and automation, commercial and delivery readiness, sector experience, and sustainability and circular production practices.
