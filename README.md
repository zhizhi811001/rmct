## About RMCT

The Regional Manufacturing Capability Taxonomy (RMCT) is a controlled vocabulary developed for manufacturing capability intelligence and innovation-to-production partner discovery.

Unlike traditional industrial classifications (e.g., SIC), RMCT captures publicly observable manufacturing capabilities described on company websites, including:

- Production process capability
- Material compatibility
- Quality, testing and certification
- Engineering support
- Digital production
- Commercial readiness
- Sector and application experience
- Sustainability practice

RMCT is designed as a faceted capability classification supporting:

- Manufacturing partner discovery
- Capability mapping
- Regional manufacturing intelligence
- Knowledge graph construction
- Innovation-to-production pathway analysis
